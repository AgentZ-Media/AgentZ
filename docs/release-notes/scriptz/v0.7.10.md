ScriptZ v0.7.10 - More reliable drag highlight, folder chips that look like folders.

## Bug fixes

- **Drag-and-drop folder highlight stays put.** When you drag a script
  onto a folder chip, the chip now stays highlighted as long as the
  cursor is anywhere over it - not just over the bottom edge. Moving
  across the label or the count no longer makes the ring flicker off
  and refuse to come back until you exit and re-enter the chip.

## Improvements

- **Folder chips look like folders.** Real folders now carry a small
  folder icon and sit on a softly raised surface, so they're visually
  distinct from the flat "All" filter chip. The row is still a tight
  strip - the focus stays on writing - but folders read as folders
  and as drop targets, not as just another tag.

## Updating

Click the green update pill in the file-browser footer to install
v0.7.10 in place.
