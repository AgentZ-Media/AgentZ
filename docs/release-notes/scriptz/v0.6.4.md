ScriptZ v0.6.4 - A short tour on first launch.

## What's new

**Onboarding on first launch**
- Four-step tour that greets you on the very first launch: Welcome,
  Theme, Character Highlighting, and Writing Defaults (daily goal +
  focus mode). Fully skippable - ✕ in the top right, skip link at
  the bottom left, or ESC.
- Step 2 lets you pick between Light, Dark, and Auto with a small
  live preview per variant.
- Step 3 shows character highlighting in a mini editor preview:
  two characters, their palette colors, a toggle - you see directly
  how your script reads with and without highlighting before
  committing.
- Step 4 sets your daily goal and focus-mode default, so you don't
  have to dive into the settings first.
- Keyboard: ↵ / → moves forward, ← back, ESC skips.
- You can restart the tour anytime via Settings → About →
  "Show onboarding again".

## Updating

Click the green update pill in the file-browser footer to install
v0.6.4 in place. The tour doesn't appear automatically - existing
installations aren't "first run" - but the restart button in the
settings is there if you want to see it.
