ScriptZ v0.6.1 - Cast stripe now shows the whole ensemble.

## What's new

**Accent stripe with real cast share**
- The colored stripe next to each script in the list, at the top of
  the grid cards, and in the "keep writing" pill now shows every
  character in the script proportionally to their dialog share. On
  top, the color of the character with the most words; below, the
  next ones - exactly the distribution the cast tab on the right of
  the editor reflects.
- Solo scripts stay single-colored, scripts without a cast stay
  subtly gray. For ensembles, every visible speaker gets at least
  8% of the stripe, so small roles stay recognizable.

## Updating

Click the green update pill in the file-browser footer to install
v0.6.1 in place. Existing scripts pick up the new proportional
stripe the next time they're saved - until then they keep their
previous look.
