ScriptZ v0.6.3 - A dedicated tab for your ideas.

## What's new

**Dedicated Ideas tab in the tab bar**
- A lightbulb tab now sits permanently next to the house icon.
  Click it to open a page that belongs only to your ideas - with a
  capture field at the top, full-text search, filters (Open / All /
  Used) and sorting (Newest / Oldest / Title).
- Click an idea to open it inline for editing - title and notes
  edit in place, `⌘+Enter` saves.
- Already-converted ideas show the link to their script: clicking
  the script name jumps right in.
- The tab carries a small pill with the number of open ideas, so
  you see at a glance how full your inbox is.
- The floating drawer at the edge of the screen and `⌘I` for quick
  capture are still there - the new page is the management home,
  the drawer is the quick tool. On the Ideas page itself the
  drawer hides, since it wouldn't add anything there.
- `⌘⌥←` / `⌘⌥→` now cycles through Home → Ideas → Scripts.

## Bug fixes

- The floating focus eye in the top right sat ever so slightly too
  high and overlapped the titlebar - now it sits cleanly below it.

## Updating

Click the green update pill in the file-browser footer to install
v0.6.3 in place. On your first click on the new lightbulb tab,
you land straight on the fresh Ideas page.
