ScriptZ v0.7.6 - Polish for the tab bar, rename flow, and trash.

## What's new

- **Tab bar breathes with many tabs.** Minimum width is up from 52 px
  to 120 px, so titles stay legible even with ten scripts open. Beyond
  that, the row scrolls horizontally instead of shrinking titles into
  nothing. Subtle vertical separators sit between tabs - the active tab
  and its left neighbor skip the line, so the "lift" stays clean.
- **Close button always sits on the right.** The ✕ used to slide next
  to the document icon on very narrow tabs and looked like a bug -
  it's now pinned reliably to the right edge of the cell.
- **Empty rename communicates itself.** Clearing the title in the
  rename dialog now shows an orange "Title cannot be empty" hint and
  disables the save button. Previously the dialog closed silently and
  the title stayed unchanged - which felt like "I just deleted the
  title".
- **Trash restore jumps back to the overview.** Restoring the last
  remaining trash entry now lands you back at your scripts instead of
  leaving you stranded in an empty trash, wondering where the script
  went. Plus a toast to confirm.
- **Streak counter speaks in words when there's nothing yet.** The
  activity modal used to print "0 writing days / 365" right under the
  big zero - doubly redundant. With zero days, it now reads "No
  writing days yet". From day one onward, the counter view is back.

## Bug fixes

- **Quick double-click on "+ New" no longer creates a phantom script.**
  Hitting the button right after closing the "New script" dialog
  occasionally produced a silent empty "Untitled". A 250 ms re-open
  guard now blocks that.
- **"Keep writing" tile no longer shows a ghost script.** Right after
  trashing a script, the tile on the overview briefly flashed the
  trashed title until the refetch finished. It's now hidden
  optimistically.

## Updating

Click the green update pill in the file-browser footer to install
v0.7.6 in place.
