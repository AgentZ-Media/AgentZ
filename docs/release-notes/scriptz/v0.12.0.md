ScriptZ v0.12.0 - Account and sync, new AI connections and smoother updates

## What's new

- **Account and sync between devices:** sign up or sign in once, connect ScriptZ, done. Your scripts, ideas, folders and settings stay in step across your devices - no codes to write down, transfer encrypted and stored encrypted in the EU. The sync status stays quiet ("Synced") and only speaks up when something needs your attention.
- **More ways to run Ida:** besides ChatGPT (Codex), Ida can now run with your own OpenRouter key - no account needed. All connections can do exactly the same: same tasks, same tools, same memory.
  - With your own key you can pick any suitable OpenRouter model from a searchable list, with a recommended default at the top.
  - Running Ida with your AgentZ account is coming soon.
- **Hide the AI completely:** if you don't want an assistant, turn it off in onboarding or in Settings > Agent. Every AI button, menu entry and shortcut disappears; your chats and memory are kept if you turn it back on.
- **Report a problem:** when signed in, a small button at the bottom right lets you tell us what went wrong in a sentence. ScriptZ attaches technical details (version, system, recent errors - never your scripts) so we can help faster. You can hide the button and bring it back in Settings > Appearance.
- **Updates without interruptions:** new versions download in the background and install when you quit, after everything is saved. ScriptZ never restarts on its own; "Restart now" is there if you want the update right away.
- **Trash empties itself:** scripts in the trash are deleted for good after 30 days, and each one shows how many days it has left.
- **Calmer lists:** folder chips on Inbox, All scripts and every stage page; a compact Inbox with the 10 newest ideas, more on request, and an optional two-column layout.
- **Better defaults:** scripts open in the large view, and character colors in the text are on unless you chose otherwise.

## Fixes

- Opening a script from a list, stage, folder or the board is instant again (it could take about 4 seconds).
- Runtimes match everywhere: Ida no longer estimates runtimes herself but uses the same numbers as the editor and the draft panel, and an empty action line no longer adds 2 seconds.
- On macOS, the first launch of a downloaded installer no longer reports the app as "damaged"; you can allow it in System Settings without using the Terminal.

## Updating

Install the update from the app's update indicator, or download the installer below. Your scripts, ideas and settings are kept.
