ScriptZ v0.10.0 - Your own writing agent and a new workspace

## What's new

- **Writing agent:** ScriptZ now has an optional personal writing agent with its own name, look and personality. It runs through the Codex CLI installed on your computer (Codex must be installed and signed in). Set it up once under Settings > Agent; while it is switched off, nothing leaves your computer.
  - **Chat per script (⌘L):** ask for ideas, rewrites or feedback. Suggestions arrive as 1 to 3 options in script style and are inserted with a click - undo with ⌘Z. The agent never writes into your script on its own.
  - **Right-click on selected text:** fact-check with web sources, talk about the passage, check the wording or rewrite it.
  - **Memory:** the agent learns your style, your folders and your characters from chats and finished scripts - always optional, and every entry can be edited or removed.
  - The chat starts closed in every script and only opens when you ask for it.
- **"Open" in the sidebar:** every script you open full-size stays in the new "Open" section until you close it, so switching between several scripts is one click. Finished scripts leave the list automatically (can be turned off).
- **Side panel:** a click in a list opens the script right next to it while the list stays visible. Expand it to the full view at any time, or turn the side panel off under Settings > Library to always open scripts full-size.
- **Board view:** Inbox, All Scripts and folders can switch between list and board. Drag a card to change its stage, or drag an idea onto a stage to turn it into a script.
- **Cleaner lists:** filter, view, grouping and sorting now sit in a toolbar right above the list. In the Inbox, scripts in progress come first and your ideas follow below.
- **New script dialog:** the yellow "New script" button (⌘N) asks for a title and a folder - you can create a new folder right there - and opens the script directly.

## Updating

Install the update from the app's update indicator, or download the installer below. Your scripts, ideas and settings are kept.
